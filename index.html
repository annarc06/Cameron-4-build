Italy & Places you could go!

The country is not just one thing you can visit and be done with. There are several locations to visit, and all have their separate quirks. Rome, the capital of Italy is a place that can never leave you bored. Florence, the medieval city housing great sculpture work and home to the infamous Medici family, whose reign via their wealth caused major discourse. Then, surrounding Florence, endless rolling hills that are just waiting for you to explore them, but if that doesn't intrigue you, then the magnificent coastline from colorful Cinque Terre to beautiful Amalfi coast, but luckily, we've narrowed it down to some highlights to save you the headache of decision. None of them pique your interest? We have more suggestions at the bottom of our page!

Rome

One gem of Italy's crown jewel and the nexus of culture, food, and fun. Some of the more notable landmarks here include the Vatican Museums & the Colosseum!

Florence

The pinnacle of culture and in the cradle of the Tuscan region, it is surrounded by rolling hills and flourishing vineyards. Wine, relaxation, Michelangelo's David, and the Uffizi Gallery's artworks await you.

Milan

The proclaimed fashion capital of Italy, it houses a variety of high-end stores and people chasing their dreams. Although, that isn't all its there for. It has some amazing (and famous) landmarks much like the others!

Venice

The final gem to our crown. This floating city is overrun with interconnecting canals, creating picturesque sights and a fantasy like environment. You can hitch a ride on their famous gondolas, indulge in their cultural food, or take a look at the Doge's Palace.

Italian Cuisine

Italy, whilst known for its history spanning centuries and all the ruins that accompany that (fun fact: Rome is often called 'lasagne' for how many layers of history and buildings it has been built upon!), it is equally known for its cuisine. Pasta is one of the most beloved dishes of the country, and something that no tourist could miss, but be sure to find an authentic restaurant before you go to any vendor. The good, authentic pasta is handmade. However, that isn't all if pasta isn't your thing. Pizza and, most importantly, Gelato, are popular and very adored by the locals.

Pasta

They have several types of pasta, but all of it tastes amazing. Why? Most of it is handmade through recipes passed down through generations. It is the birthplace of such things, and to sweeten the deal, lots of pasta cooking classes are often available!

Gelato

Gelato is a popular dessert in Italy with some of the best places being right in Rome, but commendable areas also include San Gimignano and Naples. Don't get it confused with icecream, though!

Pizza

One of Italy's signature dishes is pizza, but that's not where this cultural fanfare ends. Did you know that it's common for one to purchase a single slice of pizza and carry it with a napkin? Furthermore, most places will give you a personal pizza!

History

Italy is rich with history and culture in all its region, having housed the Roman Empire for centuries, and precluding to that, the Greeks. It is an area of paintings, sculpture, myths, and cuisine honed by many, unfathomable years. It isn't something you could possibly cover in one trip, but you can certainly know some of the more important landmarks and what they have contributed to Italy as a whole, especially when associated with the Roman Empire and their shenanigans. Did you know you can see the area where Julius Caesar was assassinated by only walking a minute out from the Colosseum?

The Colosseum

One of the most recognized landmarks in all of the country is The Colosseum, an ancient arena built to appease the masses. Did you know that the duels held in the Colosseum were often done to distract the public from political discourse?

Circus Maximus

Yet another, but slightly lesser known landmark is Circus Maximus. It was a racetrack for chariots that backed right up to the palace. Did you know that there were varying teams? And that one emperor gained disfavor among his council since he showed too much devotion to one team?

The Vasari Corridor

This tunnel was designed by the order of the Medici family to connect the Palazzo Vecchio to the Palazzo Pitti, so that they may move discreetly from location to location. It is one of many that this illustrious family utilized in their time of power.

Activities

For first time travelers, figuring out where to go is tough but what to do? That's an entirely different ballgame. Fortunately, Italy has long since been in the travel business and has ways to keep you entertained, but it depends on where you go. Most areas offer similar activities, especially the in-land ones with day trips run by businesses, walking tours, and much more at your finger tips. For the coastal areas, boat tours, scuba diving, and beach clubs might entice you, but here are just a few activities you can consider otherwise.

Cooking Class

Ever wanted to learn how to make pasta the Italian way? What about other dishes like gelato or heart-shaped pizza? Italy, especially in Florence, has a wide-ranged of expert chefs and people who've mastered the craft of Italian cuisine eager to teach. You could be the next pupil!

Golf cart Ride

For those who can't or simply don't want to trek up the steps of Rome and many other spots, a golf cart tour is for you. It's easy, breezy, and you can even decide where you want to go in most cases. Equally, it's perfect for those who don't want to corral kids during hikes around the city.

Museums

This is certainly an obvious one, but you can't visit Italy without at least seeing one famous painting or one marble statue! It would be in bad taste. Whilst there are many to see, some of the most notable come from Florence (David's Michelangelo) or from Rome, such as in the Borghese Gallery.
